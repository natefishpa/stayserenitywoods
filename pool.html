The short version

We handle the upkeep. You handle the floats.

The pool is serviced and chemically balanced for you before every stay, and [our pool service visits on [day of week] during the season]. You should never need to add chemicals, backwash a filter, or adjust equipment.

What we do ask: keep the surface clear of obvious debris, put the cover back on when the pool is not in use, and tell us right away if the water or the equipment looks wrong. That is the whole job.

Skimmer basket

The skimmer is [location]. If you see leaves or bugs collecting, lift the lid and empty the basket into the yard waste — it takes ten seconds and keeps the water clear. [Add a note if guests should not open it at all.]

Pump and filter

The pump runs on a timer, so you will hear it cycle on and off. Please do not switch it off, unplug it, or change the timer — circulation is what keeps the water clean and safe.

Chemicals and testing

Please do not add chlorine, shock, algaecide, or anything else to the pool. The chemistry is managed for you, and guest-added product is the most common cause of a pool going cloudy mid-stay. [Note where test strips are kept, if you want guests to have them.]

Water level

The water should sit about [halfway up the skimmer opening]. Evaporation and splash-out will drop it over a hot week. [If guests may top it off: "The hose at [location] can be used to add water — run it for about [20 minutes] and turn it off."] If the level drops fast, tell us.

Toys, floats, and furniture

[List what is provided — noodles, floats, skimmer net, lounge chairs — and where it is stored.] Please rinse and return everything to [storage location] at the end of your stay so the next guests find it the way you did.

After a storm

Summer storms roll through this valley fast. Get everyone out of the water at the first thunder, move cushions and light furniture under cover, and wait [30 minutes] after the last thunder before going back in. Debris in the water after a storm is normal — the skimmer will catch most of it within a few hours.

Troubleshooting

If something looks wrong.

Most of these are normal and fix themselves. Message us either way — we would rather hear about it twice than not at all.

Water looks cloudy

Usually a heavy swim day, sunscreen, or a passing storm. The filter typically clears it within [24 hours]. Do not add anything to the water — just let us know.

Water looks green

Not normal. Stop swimming and message us right away so we can get service out. [Add your preferred contact route.]

Pool is cold

Check that the cover was on overnight, then give the heater [a few hours] to recover. If it is still cold after that, tell us — it may need a reset we can do remotely.

Pump is loud or silent

The pump cycling on and off is normal. Grinding, screeching, or complete silence during daytime hours is not. Do not open the equipment — message us.

Water level dropped

A few inches over a hot week is evaporation. Several inches overnight is not. [Top-off instructions or "message us" — your call.]

Something broke

Accidents happen and we would much rather know immediately than find it at turnover. Message us through the booking platform with a photo and we will sort it out.
