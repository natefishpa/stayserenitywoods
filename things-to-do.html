Close to home

Under twenty minutes from the driveway.

You can do any of these and still be back at the pool by mid-afternoon.

Fishing

There is a lot of good local fishing, starting with Cowanesque Lake about a mile from the house. Bring the rods and you will find water within a few minutes in almost any direction.

Golf

Seventeen minutes away is Indian Hills Golf Club in Painted Post, NY — 18 holes, open to the public, with a driving range and restaurant. Tee times can be booked on their site.

Gun range

Eight minutes away is the Cowanesque Valley Rod & Gun Club in Elkland, with both rifle and pistol ranges. Membership runs about $35 for a full year — no per-visit fees, unlimited shooting while you are there, and you can go every single day of your trip at no extra cost.

Plan ahead: sign up on their site at least three weeks before your stay so your membership card arrives in time.

Hiking

The property itself is good for wandering — eight acres of woods, creek frontage, and room to walk. A few minutes' drive gets you to the Moccasin Trail, an easy out-and-back along the Cowanesque Lake shoreline with lake and hillside views.

For something bigger, Watkins Glen State Park is 42 minutes north — the gorge trail there is the one everyone photographs.

Hunting

There is no hunting on the property, but there are plenty of options nearby. Tioga Boar Hunting is about fifteen minutes away and runs guided hunts year-round.

Food delivery

Delivery options are thin this far out. The one place we have found that will bring food to the property is The Pizza Barn in Elkland — we like their pizza, and they do Mexican dishes as well. For anything else, plan on a pickup run or a drive.

Corning & Elmira

Twenty-five minutes to a proper night out.

Corning is the nearest town with real evening options, and Elmira adds theatre and live music another fifteen minutes past it. This is where to go when the weather turns or the kids need something to do.

Corning Museum of Glass

The home of Corning Ware, twenty-five minutes northeast. Tour the museum, watch a glassblowing demonstration, or book a make-your-own-glass project and take it home with you. The best rainy-day plan in the region and genuinely good with kids.

The Gaffer District

Corning's historic downtown — food, drinks, museums, shops, antiques, and a steady calendar of events. Worth an afternoon on its own. See what's on in the Gaffer District.

Outdoor fun park

Park Avenue Sports Center in Corning has laser tag, mini golf, batting cages, a rock wall, a bungee jump, a driving range, and water wars. They also do burgers, hot dogs, and sixty flavors of ice cream — so you can make a whole evening of it without going anywhere else.

Movie theater

Spotlight Theatres in Corning — check showtimes before you drive over.

Indoor golf

Tee Time runs indoor golf simulators with locations near Corning and in Elmira. Book a bay by the hour — up to four people per bay — and play courses from around the world or just hit the virtual range. A good rainy-day or winter option.

Performing arts

The Clemens Center in Elmira brings in Broadway tours, concerts, comedy, and family shows. Check the season calendar against your dates.

Orchestra

The Orchestra of the Southern Finger Lakes performs throughout the region — worth a look if your stay lines up with a concert.

Worth the drive

Day trips in every direction.

Twenty-five minutes to an hour, and each one can fill an afternoon or a whole day.

The Pennsylvania Grand Canyon

About thirty minutes south is the PA Grand Canyon — Pine Creek Gorge, forty-five miles long and nearly 1,500 feet deep. Overlooks at Leonard Harrison and Colton Point, plus the Pine Creek Rail Trail for biking.

Waterfalls

Watkins Glen gets the attention, but it is far from the only one — the region is full of gorges and falls within an easy drive. Open the regional waterfall map to pick a route.

Finger Lakes wine tours

Call Scooter's party bus at (570) 418-0013 and he will drive you around for eight hours — wineries, food stops, wherever you want to go. Up to nine people for about $240 total, so split between three, four, six or nine of you it is genuinely cheap. We have heard excellent things about this service.

Plan your stops on the Seneca Lake Wine Trail.

Charter fishing on Seneca Lake

Five hours on the water for up to four people with a local captain — lake trout, rainbow, brown trout and salmon depending on the season. Book a charter.

Kayak & paddleboard rentals

Same outfitter rents kayaks, canoes and paddleboards if you would rather be the one doing the paddling. See rental options.

Watkins Glen International

One of the great American road courses. Race weekends, track days and events run through the season — check the schedule at The Glen and see if your dates line up.

Cherry Springs stargazing

One of the darkest skies on the East Coast, and worth the evening drive if the forecast is clear. Cherry Springs State Park — check their dark-sky program schedule and moon phase before you go.

Wellsboro at Christmas

Every December, Wellsboro puts on Dickens of a Christmas — gas-lit streets, period costumes, and one of the best small-town holiday festivals in the state. Worth planning a winter stay around.

Casino

If you have the itch, Tioga Downs is about an hour east — casino, harness racing, and restaurants.
